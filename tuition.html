How tuition works

One all-inclusive monthly payment covers tuition, costumes and recital costs, so there are no surprise bills in May.

All-inclusive, on purpose

Many studios quote tuition only, then add registration, costume and recital fees later in the year. We build those into one plan and split it into equal monthly payments, charged on the 1st of each month to a card on file.

That means our monthly number can look higher than a tuition-only quote, but it’s the whole cost of the semester, not part of it.

ContractPayments

Your total depends on your dancer’s level and how many classes they take. A person on our team builds every quote.

Estimated monthly payment

$105–$210/month

Typical for dancers taking 30 minutes to 2 hours of class a week. Teams and extra hours cost more.

These are estimates only, not a quote. Your exact price is built by our team.

More classes, lower hourly rate

Each additional hour of class time is added at a reduced rate. The more your dancer takes, the less you pay per hour.

Included in one price

Everything below is built into your monthly payment.

  • Registration
  • Tuition
  • Recital costume for each class
  • Recital fee
  • Recital video download
  • Recital T-shirt
  • Recital program (one per family)
  • Class group photo download
  • Parade participation

Not included: uniform items and shoes, recital tickets, snack bar and water, additional photos and recital merch.

How to enroll

  1. Tell us about your dancer

    Fill out the form or text us.

  2. We select best-fit classes

    Based on age, experience and interests.

  3. Get your quote

    One all-inclusive monthly price, built by our team.

  4. Book your enrollment appointment

    This covers your tour and uniform fitting.

  5. Join us for class!

    We’ll see you in the studio.

Paying and managing your account

Invoices come by email through Helcim, and families keep a card on file. You can view your payment history and update your card anytime in the family portal. Full policies, including withdrawals and late fees, are in the Policies & Fees Handbook.
