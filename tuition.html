How tuition works

One all-inclusive monthly payment covers tuition, costumes and recital costs, so there are no surprise bills in May.

Included in one price

Everything below is built into your monthly payment.

  • Registration
  • Tuition
  • Recital costume for each class
  • Recital fee
  • Recital video download
  • Recital T-shirt
  • Recital program (one per family)
  • Class group photo download
  • Parade participation

Not included: uniform items and shoes, recital tickets, snack bar and water, extra photos and recital merch.

All-inclusive, on purpose

Many studios quote tuition only, then add registration, costume and recital fees later in the year. We build all of it into one plan, split into equal monthly payments.

Our monthly number can look higher than a tuition-only quote. That’s because it’s the whole cost of the semester, not part of it.
