What happens next

Five quick steps. We move fast so your dancer can start sooner.

  1. Tell us about your dancer

    Fill out the form or text us.

  2. We select best-fit classes

    Based on age, experience and interests.

  3. Get your quote

    One all-inclusive monthly price.

  4. Book your enrollment appointment

    Your tour and uniform fitting.

  5. Join us for class!

    We’ll see you in the studio.

Programs at a glance

Classes for ages 1.5 and up in eight styles. Most new dancers start at the level for their age.

  • Dance With Me1.5 to 4
  • Intro3 to 5
  • Peewee6 to 9
  • Level 19 to 12+
  • Level 212+

Semester classes

Our most popular program. One or two semesters of technique and choreography, ending with every class on stage in its own costume.

Five teams

By audition, from local performances to full competition, including Team Wildfire, our adaptive inclusive team.

About $105–$210/month

Estimated all-inclusive monthly payment for most dancers. Our team builds your exact quote.
