A class for every age

Dancers move up as they grow. Placement is a starting point, and our teachers make the final call so every dancer is challenged without being overwhelmed.

  1. Glow1.5 to 4Dance With Me

    Brand-new walkers dance alongside a parent or caregiver.

  2. Ember3 to 5Intro to Dance

    First classes on their own: following directions, motor skills, friends.

  3. Spark6 to 9Peewee

    45-minute style classes. Most dancers try two or more styles.

  4. Scorch9 to 12+Level 1

    More technique, longer combinations, more corrections.

  5. Blaze12+Level 2

    By audition, for focused, self-motivated dancers.

What we teach

Every class works on technique first, then learns a piece of choreography to perform at the end of the semester.

Ballet

The foundation for every other style: placement, core strength and the French vocabulary dance is built on.

Acro

Dance plus acrobatics: balances, flexibility, tumbling and partnering, taught on the Acrobatic Arts syllabus.

Jazz

High-energy, commercial jazz with leaps, turns and tricks.

Hip Hop

Street style built on grooves, isolations and freedom of movement.

Tap

Rhythm you can hear. Musicality, counting and making sound together.

Lyrical

Ballet technique with room to breathe, telling the story of a song.

Musical Theatre

Dance, acting and singing together, with character work and vocal warm-ups.

Dance Tech

Strength and alignment training with exercise balls and therabands.
