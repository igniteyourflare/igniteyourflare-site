A class for every age

Dancers move up as they grow. Placement is a starting point, and our teachers make the final call so every dancer is challenged without being overwhelmed.

  1. Glow1.5 to 4Dance With Me

    Brand-new walkers dance alongside a parent or caregiver.

  2. Ember3 to 5Intro to Dance

    First classes on their own: following directions, motor skills, friends.

  3. Spark6 to 9Peewee

    45-minute style classes. Most dancers try two or more styles.

  4. Scorch9 to 12+Level 1

    More technique, longer combinations, more corrections.

  5. Blaze12+Level 2

    By audition, for focused, self-motivated dancers.

What we teach

Every class works on technique first, then learns a piece of choreography to perform at the end of the semester.

Ballet

The foundation for every other style: placement, core strength and the French vocabulary dance is built on.

Acro

Dance plus acrobatics: balances, flexibility, tumbling and partnering, taught on the Acrobatic Arts syllabus.

Jazz

High-energy, commercial jazz with leaps, turns and tricks, the style you see on So You Think You Can Dance.

Hip Hop

Street style built on grooves, isolations and freedom of movement. Hard-hitting, aerobic and fun.

Tap

Rhythm you can hear. Dancers learn musicality, counting and making sound together as a team.

Lyrical

Ballet technique with room to breathe, telling the story of a song through movement and expression.

Musical Theatre

Dance, acting and singing together: character work, vocal warm-ups and lip-sync to choreography.

Dance Tech

Strength and alignment training with exercise balls and therabands. Mixed levels welcome.

Why families stay

We believe in Commitment, Community and Character, and we teach them in every class alongside technique. Teachers know every dancer by name, hold them to high standards, and celebrate every small win.

  • Acrobatic Arts certified. Our staff trained on the international AcroDance syllabus for safe skill progressions.
  • Team Wildfire, our adaptive inclusive team, supports dancers with disabilities, neurodivergence, learning differences or medical needs.
  • Two real performances a year at the State Theatre, with every class in its own costume.
  • All-inclusive tuition. Costumes, recital fee, video and class photo are built into one monthly payment.
  • No waiting room needed. Parents can watch class live online, and the studio has cameras throughout.

Recreational or competitive, your choice

Most dancers take semester classes and perform in two recitals a year. Dancers who want more can audition for one of five teams, from local performances to full competition.
