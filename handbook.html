Philosophy, Personal Responsibilities and Commitment

Please note that this document is legally binding and is part of our terms of service. We need Clients and Students to review these terms together.

At Ignite Your Flare each and every student is an important member of our studio. We believe performers should be taught in a safe, nurturing, and positive environment that focuses on attention to detail and proper technique.

PERFORMANCE ART IS NOT LEARNED OVERNIGHT. The average performer spends upwards of 15 years learning their craft before being considered proficient and decades more for mastery. Professional dancers continue their training on and off stage throughout their careers. We believe in creating a solid foundation, celebrating success (no matter how small), and supporting our students in their times of hardship. The art of performance takes hard work, dedication, and tenacity. We will laugh and cry, but we will do it as a team.

Training as a performer is a major commitment. We require a minimum of a semester commitment from all students enrolling with us for our Fall and/or Spring semesters. We believe that this time period is sufficient to establish whether you are interested in pursuing performance art seriously. When you enroll you are reserving a space in our class that cannot be filled by anyone else.

For Students

  • Be punctual, dressed and ready to go 5 minutes before class begins, waiting quietly.
  • Follow directions.
  • Respect yourself, teachers, parents, and peers.
  • Work hard in every class.
  • Stay Humble, Show Gratitude, & Always be THANKFUL.
  • Prepare your own dance bag (daily)
  • Learn to do your own hair correctly, check that you have supplies with you every time. (7+yrs)
  • Practice your own makeup (7+yrs)
  • Update your parents & teachers: shoes not fitting, new tights, events…
  • Eat, sleep, practice healthy lifestyle habits. Listen to your body. Well-being comes first.
  • PRACTICE (until your confident enough to perform with precision)
  • Do your homework.
  • Practice Good Hygiene: Shower after every class/event day. Wear deodorant, re-apply as needed. Help make sure your dance clothes are clean & tidy
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct, and/or gossip will not be tolerated.
  • Communicate. Keep your teachers up-to-date, ask for help.
  • Show up for yourself and your team. Attend every class in person (or when necessary, online). Always let us know when and why you need to miss before you miss. If you do miss make sure you practice before the next class by reviewing any available videos.
  • Follow through with your commitment to yourself, teachers, and fellow students.

Attendance & Illnesses

Consistent attendance is extremely important for your student’s success and the success of the class.

If your student needs to miss class for any reason, we ask that you inform the front office via your BAND chat BEFORE the missed class.

If your student is missing class due to contagious illness, please inform the front desk immediately. If your student had a fever and was present in class within 24hrs of the fever’s onset.

Keep your student home if they have/had within 24hrs:

  • Fever greater than 99 degrees
  • Diarrhea
  • Vomiting
  • A wet cough
  • Rash (of unknown origin)
  • Head Lice
  • Pink Eye
  • Or any other major symptom of contagious illness.

Students may return to class in person once they have been symptom free for 24 hours or with a doctor’s note detailing the condition and clearance to return.

If a student is falling behind, due to a pattern of absences they may be required to book, pay for, and attend private lessons to be able to participate in recital, out of fairness to their classmates. Classes are most often made available for review via recordings available in each respective class Band. Students who miss classes are responsible for reviewing the missed materials before the next class meeting and keeping up with the rest of the class. In the event that a class recording is not available, it is the student’s responsibility to speak with the teacher about the missed materials BEFORE the next class meeting. Private solo lessons are available ONLY if a teacher or director offers to provide them.

Class Etiquette

Be on Time. Warm-up time is crucial to avoid injury! Students must arrive prepared and on time for all classes. If a student is more than 10 minutes late or unprepared for a class they will be permitted to sit and watch only. Repeat offenders may be asked to leave until such time as they are ready to attend class respectfully and are prepared.

No food or drink in the dance room, except water, or approved snacks during break. Please take your trash home with you and clean out your dance bag regularly. Our back room has a vending machine available with approved snacks for break times, please send your students with change to class if they would like to utilize the vending machine.

Cell phones are not to be used in class and should be turned off or on mute. NO PHOTOGRAPHY OR VIDEOS (this is a liability issue). Unless you request specific permission from your teacher and those being photographed to do so.

Keep hands off the mirrors. No hanging on the barres. Leave corrections to the teacher to handle, unless otherwise invited.

Watch Policy

Students ONLY in the classroom space. We do not have a waiting/watch room. We believe that independence is a major key to success in life and as a performer and that having a class environment free from distractions is essential to the learning process. This policy will be strictly enforced.

We offer a live-streaming option for clients to be able to view online during class. If you choose to pop-in to view online, we must have you turn off both your video & audio for the duration of your viewing so as to not disrupt the recording. Links to classes are made available via individual class Bands.

Please do not interrupt class unless there is an emergency. If your student needs to be excused early for any reason please inform the teacher BEFORE class begins. At the time you need to leave, stand at the door and the teacher will excuse your student.

Students dropped off early/left after hours/past their classes ending time (greater than 5 minutes) will be subject to a $1 per minute babysitting fee. If a student is left after hours for more than 15 minutes the authorities may be called if a guardian is unreachable.

Code of Conduct

Students and Clients will conduct themselves in an appropriate manner while at Ignite Your Flare and at all performances and events in which Ignite Your Flare is being represented. Proper respect for instructors, space, and peers is expected of all students, Clients/guardians, and guests.

Bring a good attitude to class. Attitudes that are disrespectful, uncooperative, or aggressive do not have a place at Ignite Your Flare. We believe in the growth mindset, you can do anything with creativity, time, patience, and perseverance.

Any Client/guardian or student who disrupts class or approaches a teacher, another student, or client with the intent to cause dissent or contravene the policies and procedures of Ignite Your Flare will be dismissed immediately and legal action may follow. The card on file will be charged for any remaining balance and semester commitments.

Ignite Your Flare values the well-being and respect of its staff. Should the Client display behavior deemed rude, abusive, or uncooperative toward any member of Ignite Your Flare’s staff or other Clients, Ignite Your Flare reserves the right, at its sole discretion, to terminate this agreement immediately. In the event of such termination, the Client shall not be entitled to any refund of fees or any other charges paid under this agreement, nor shall the Client be entitled to any benefits, guarantees, or warranties that may have been provided or promised under this agreement.

The use of social media outlets are commonplace. This policy is intended to provide Clients and students with guidelines for the use of social media regarding Ignite Your Flare.

Please use common sense and discretion when posting on these sites. Never post anything that could compromise the self-esteem/safety of students who attend Ignite Your Flare. Never post any negative comments about Ignite Your Flare, our teachers, our performances, or any events attended. Any inappropriate or slanderous remarks about Ignite Your Flare on any social media site will be cause for immediate dismissal from the studio. Legal action may follow. If you have a problem let us know via email/BAND and we will do our best to help correct the issue. Always discuss any issues you may have with a staff member immediately, either in person at an appropriate time, via BAND private message, or via email to office@igniteyourflare.com. Often, frustration arises with the failure to communicate effectively, we are always willing to clarify our policies and reasoning. No videos or pictures are to be taken or shared while at Ignite Your Flare or Ignite Your Flare events without specific case-by-case consent from the Ignite Your Flare director.

Do not contact the Ignite Your Flare staff or teachers through their personal social media pages. Please utilize your BAND chat for all communications as the proper way to get a hold of any staff or get your questions answered.

If it is brought to our attention that there has been an improper use of social media, you will be asked to remove the content if deemed egregious, and if not done so or if this continues to be a problem it may result in dismissal of the student with full payment due upon dismissal and/or legal action.

Dress Code, Uniforms, & Supplies

ALL DANCEWEAR/PERFORMANCE ITEMS MUST BE ORDERED FROM Ignite Your Flare! If your student forgets or is missing an item necessary to take class on more than one occasion, and it is in stock, a staff member will give the student the item and your account will be charged accordingly.

Students must wear appropriate attire or they will not be allowed to participate. Hair must be pulled into a tidy and solid bun that will last the entire length of instruction. (Please, no hair donuts or accessories) If your student’s hair needs to be fixed during instruction a bun kit will be provided and charged to your account.

All clothing should be cleaned between each class day and free of major holes. Having more than one uniform is HIGHLY suggested for students attending multiple days per week or from split households. All items should be labeled with the student’s name on the tag/inside of the article. All students must wear deodorant. DANCE SHOES ARE TO BE WORN IN THE STUDIO ONLY! Shoes must be changed upon entering the building and before exiting. Dance shoes are NEVER to be worn outside. Hip Hop may utilize our Flare basketball shorts and tank top or other Ignite Your Flare warm-up items, such as an Ignite Your Flare T-Shirt. These items may be able to be utilized in other classes, but can be requested to be removed at teacher’s discretion. Acro specifically would be required to have a leotard underneath items for safety of students during acrobatic maneuvers.

Ignite Your Flare logo wear is the intellectual property of our studio. As such we will not allow “home” created Ignite Your Flare logos, lettering, or the like. Do not purchase iron on logos or create your own to represent the studio in any way without explicit approval from the director. Sales of unsanctioned apparel of this kind are strictly prohibited and legal action may follow. We rely on the sales of Ignite Your Flare logo wear as income to keep our doors open. We appreciate your cooperation and support for our local small business.

There are many reasons behind our strict dress code policy:

  • Safety
  • When we look like a team, we act as a team.
  • Uniformity helps us teach more efficiently and effectively.
  • This isn’t a fashion show or contest, no bullying around wearing major brands, etc.
  • Helps to lessen costs for performances when we know who has the “basic” items when we need them.
  • Ambassadors wearing our logo should be responsible for their actions when representing our studio.
  • Our uniforms are designed for all shapes and sizes.

All students MUST have: (labeled with the student’s name inside)

  • Ignite Your Flare Leotard & Ignite Your Flare Shorts
  • Ignite Your Flare approved tan stirrup tights. PLEASE NOTE: Tights are underwear, do not wear additional underwear with tights.
  • Ignite Your Flare approved appropriate dance shoes for each class style.
  • Ignite Your Flare logo-wear ONLY may be used for warm-ups.
  • Acro Requires Additional Items: Yoga Mat, Theraband, Yoga Blocks, Theraloop, Theraband
  • Dance Tech Additional Items: Yoga Mat, Yoga Blocks, Theraloop, Theraband, Tennis Ball

Orders can usually be filled within two weeks, we will not place standard orders until we have a full order. Rush orders (anything less than 2 weeks notice) are subject to rush shipping fees. Orders must be paid in full before we place your order. We do not offer refunds so please triple check that your order is correct before submitting it to us & picking up your items. If your student takes home any store item then the corresponding account will be charged and processed as soon as possible by a staff member. Sales tax will be charged on all applicable items. Please note that in prime costume/recital seasons shipping often takes an additional 1-2 weeks. PLEASE make sure to let us know EARLY if your student is in need of replacement items (especially shoes) and try to think ahead for their needs, especially in preparation for recital at the end of each semester.

You have a 14 day grace period (or the time needed for the company to process the order) from the time of enrollment to be dressed appropriately. After this period you will not be allowed to take any class and will only be allowed to sit and watch until you have the correct items for dress code. If you forget your uniform you will not be able to participate, unless we have replacement items in stock, in which case they will be charged to your account. If replacement items are not available, you will be permitted only to sit and watch.

Recitals

We offer many different performance opportunities at Ignite Your Flare. Recitals and community performances are mandatory for all students enrolled in our seasonal program for all applicable classes.

Performing is an exceptionally important part of a performance education. Students learn a very different set of skills from regular classes in the weeks leading up to the performance and while performing. Students involved in recitals will participate in a Stage Craft & Performance Preparation Class during Tech Week (the week before recital) which will often differ from standard class times and content.

Other opportunities include parades, and other local events. Our specialty teams are a great way to be involved in even more performance opportunities, ask the front desk or message us for more information & how to audition!

Stage Makeup

A requirement for all performance and photo events. This consists of heavy “normal” makeup. Eyeliner, maroon/red lipstick, blush, mascara, darkened eyebrows (for those with light brows), brown eye shadow are all required for ALL performers. (This is to create not only a polished look, but to also ensure that students do not look sickly / washed out / or ghostly.) Some performers may be required additional character makeup that will be set along with costumes. Makeup and hair supplies are unique to each student and should compliment their complexion and be used solely by them for hygiene reasons. Hair supplies must match the current color of the student’s hair.

Costumes

A required part of the performance as well as personal items such as undergarments (tights and nude leotards), required shoes, hair, and makeup, which are not included in tuition, recital, or costume fees. Every performer must have and wear a skin-tone “nude” leotard for all performances. Shoes may differ from the shoes worn in standard classes. If a student is found to be missing any of the required items, they will be ordered and charged to your account immediately.

Costume fees are assessed per performing class style. It is our goal to create costumes that are reasonably economical while serving their intended purpose for each performer/show in a functional manner. Additional accessory, seamstress, and cleaning fees may apply as needed.

Costumes are the property of the studio until the conclusion of all performance commitments. All costumes must be returned to the studio after recital. In efforts to keep costs to a minimum, costumes/accessories may not be kept by the student and will be considered a rental. If this is the case, you will be notified and credited accordingly. Rental costume credits expire after the following semester the credits were accounted for. If your student is no longer enrolled in Ignite Your Flare for the following semester, rental costume credits will be absorbed into our scholarship fund. NO REFUNDS will be applied for rental costume credits.

The client understands that all rental costumes remain the property of Ignite Your Flare and must be returned in the same condition in which they were received, excluding normal wear from approved performance use.

The client accepts full financial responsibility for any damage to a rental costume occurring while it is in their possession. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Makeup stains
  • Food or beverage stains
  • Dirt, mud, or grass stains
  • Deodorant, self-tanner, hairspray, or other cosmetic residue
  • Rips, tears, snags, or holes
  • Broken or missing zippers, hooks, closures, buttons, or embellishments
  • Missing costume pieces or accessories
  • Any other damage beyond normal wear and tear

If a costume is returned with damage requiring professional cleaning or repair, the renter's/student's account will be charged the full cost of the required cleaning and/or repair.

If the costume is determined by Ignite Your Flare to be damaged beyond reasonable repair, lost, or otherwise unusable for future performances, the renter's/student's account will be charged the full replacement value of the costume. Ensure that your costumes are properly checked out at the end of each show.

All repair and replacement determinations, including associated costs, shall be made at the sole discretion of Ignite Your Flare. Charges will be applied to the student's account and are due in accordance with the studio's payment policies.

Pick-up for non-rental costumes will be during the first week of the following semester (for Fall costumes- the first week of Spring classes, for Spring costumes- the first week of Summer classes). Costumes/Lost & Found items not picked up within the first week of classes following recital will be forfeited to the studio, no credits or refunds will be applied.

If you fail to complete your commitment to your studio and do not perform in recital or performance- you will not receive a refund or any costumes paid for they become the property of the studio. Furthermore, you may be placed on probation for participation in further events. (Probation: All fees, recital, and tuition, are due on the first day of classes- no payment options. Not eligible for “lead roles” for one semester.) Not showing up to performances affects everyone involved and creates complications for other students' performances and choreography. Please pay close attention to recital dates and ensure you are available for these dates.

Recital Commitment & Tickets

If any recital holds less than 40 students it will be held at Ignite Your Flare or another small local institution and each student recital participant is only guaranteed 2 tickets in the pre-sale ticket time period. If the student fails to purchase their 2 tickets before the pre-sale deadline they are no longer guaranteed any tickets.

Tickets may only be purchased once the student’s account is up to date. Tickets may only be picked up once the students’ account is paid off for the remainder of the semester, including all late fees, incidentals, and etc. All attendees must have a ticket to watch the performance for EACH show; i.e. one for the morning and one for the evening show. Infants in arms are an exception.

Generally, we aim to run at least 2 shows within the same day/weekend. All dancers are required to perform in all show times. Students performing in the show (including Dance With Me Clients) do not require the purchase of a ticket. Dance With Me and Intro to Dance level students are considered lap sitters and will not be assigned their own seat. If you would like them to have their own seat, they can sit in the designated student area with our Client volunteers or you can purchase their own seat next to you.

No Videos or Photography are permitted in any recital. All content is the intellectual property of Ignite Your Flare and is professionally captured for purchase, unless otherwise stated.

STUDENTS MUST PARTICIPATE IN ALL SHOWTIMES FOR EVERY RECITAL COMMITMENT!

After enrollment, families are expected to review and reference the Policies & Fees Handbook, website, client portal, emails, and other official studio communications before contacting staff with questions. We are always happy to clarify policies or explain how they apply to your individual situation.

If a family repeatedly requests staff assistance regarding information that has already been provided in writing and previously explained, Ignite Your Flare reserves the right to assess an Administrative Support Fee to compensate for excessive staff time devoted to repeated policy review.

Administrative Support Fees may be assessed as follows:

  • First and second occurrences: Courtesy reminder and reference to the applicable policy.

  • Fourth occurrence: $25 Administrative Support Fee.

Families who would like additional one-on-one assistance are encouraged to schedule an administrative appointment. We are committed to helping every family succeed while ensuring our staff's time remains available to provide exceptional service and instruction to all students.

Late Fees, Chargebacks, Failed Payments and Collections

Past-due accounts may be charged 5% of outstanding balance OR $25.00 (whichever is greater) per billing cycle. Late fees reflect administrative and processing costs and are not considered a penalty.

Returned or failed payments (insufficient funds, expired cards, chargebacks, declined transactions) may incur a $25 Returned Payment Fee per occurrence, any applicable late fees, and immediate retry of stored payment method.
