Why placement matters

The right challenge

A dancer in a level that’s too high or too low gets frustrated.

Safety first

In classes like acro, a dancer who hasn’t mastered the basics can put themselves and others at risk.

Teacher’s call

This chart is a general guide. Final placement is at the teacher’s discretion, and we sometimes ask a dancer to try a class first.
