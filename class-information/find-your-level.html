Which dance class is right for my child?

Start with your dancer’s age and experience. If they’re on the edge between two levels, a trial class settles it.

AgeNew to danceHas experience
1.5 to 2Dance With MeDance With Me
3 to 4Dance With Me or Intro to DanceIntro to Dance
5Intro to DanceIntro to Dance or Peewee
6 to 8PeeweePeewee
9Level 1Level 1
10 to 11Level 1Level 1
12+Level 1Level 1, or Level 2 by audition

An 8-year-old may audition directly into Level 1.

Why placement matters

A dancer in a level that’s too high or too low gets frustrated. In some classes, like acro, safety is the bigger concern: a dancer who hasn’t mastered the basics can put themselves and others at risk. That’s why we sometimes ask a dancer to try a class first.

This chart is a general guide and won’t fit every dancer. Final class placement is at the teacher’s discretion. Questions? Text us and we’ll help.

Not sure where to start? Tell us about your dancer.

We reply with the best-fit class and the current schedule, usually within one business day.
