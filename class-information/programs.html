Semester classes

This is the program most of our dancers choose. Each semester is about five months focused on the styles you sign up for. The first half builds technique, and the second half learns and polishes a piece of choreography.

At the end of the semester every class performs its own dance, in its own costume, at our recital. A dancer in ballet and tap, for example, has two costumes and performs in both dances. It’s a great way to see how much your child grows in five months.

About recitals

Teams are a commitment

Team dancers commit to the full season, including outside rehearsals, extra performances and additional costs. Each team has required classes, and we encourage competition dancers to take every style since convention classes cover them all.

We look for events within about four hours of Red Bluff. They usually run a full weekend, sometimes starting Thursday or Friday night. Team dancers are ambassadors for Flare: kind, helpful, prepared and positive.

Our teams

Team GlowLocal team

Perfect for dancers who want more stage time without traveling. Glow performs at local events like Spooktacular, the Children’s Christmas Festival and Tough Enough to Wear Pink. Dancers learn what it means to be part of a team, gain stage experience and grow their confidence.

Team EmberTraveling, non-competitive

A way to dip your toes into competition life. Ember dancers attend one out-of-town event for a weekend of convention classes with master choreographers, cheer on teammates and watch other studios compete, without the pressure of competing themselves.

Team WildfireAdaptive inclusive team

Designed to be inclusive and supportive of dancers with disabilities, neurodivergence, learning differences, medical needs, or any circumstance that calls for individualized adaptations. Like Ember, one out-of-town event is required and others are optional.

Team SparkPre-team

The bridge between Ember and Blaze. Spark dancers attend one out-of-town event and compete one dance there, with the option to add more if available. We encourage them to attend more events for convention classes and to cheer on teammates.

Team BlazeFull competitive team

Our team with the most commitment. Blaze dancers attend all local and competitive events, perform at least two routines with the option to add a solo or more group routines, and meet a class requirement at Flare. They’re held to a high standard as leaders at the studio and at events.
