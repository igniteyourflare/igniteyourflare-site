Support our team dancers

Every team dancer trains, travels and competes all season. Your gift helps them chase their dreams and represent Red Bluff with pride.

Meet the dancersBusiness sponsorships

How to give

Support a dancer with a Flare gift card in their name. Gift cards are not tax-deductible.

  1. Choose a dancer

    Read their story and season goal below.

  2. Tap “Support”

    You’ll go to our secure studio checkout to buy a gift card in any amount.

  3. Add their name

    Write the dancer’s name under “Add special instructions” so your gift reaches them.
