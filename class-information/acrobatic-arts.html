Acro dance classes, Acrobatic Arts certified

Acro blends classical dance technique with the precision and athleticism of acrobatics, taught on the world’s most widely used AcroDance syllabus.

What is acro?

AcroDance, or acro, fuses dance technique with acrobatic elements. Acro dancers blend musicality, emotional expression, line and extension with acrobatic movement, all in a dance context.

Acro has softer, more lyrical lines than gymnastics. We teach dancers to move into and out of tricks with minimal obvious preparation, so the tricks blend into the choreography and add excitement to it.

It’s a challenging style because it requires training in both dance and acrobatics: balancing, limbering, tumbling and partnering, built on flexibility, contortion and strength.

Gymnastics vs. acro

GymnasticsAcro
FloorSpring floor, higher jumps and softer landingsHard or sprung floor; dancers train to create their own lift
EquipmentBeam, bars and moreFloor only
PerformersUsually one at a timeSolo, partner or group

Certified studio

Acrobatic Arts certified

Ignite Your Flare is an Acrobatic Arts certified studio. Our instructors completed Acrobatic Arts’ international curriculum training, so every dancer gets safe, effective skill progressions toward their own goals.

Acrobatic Arts is the industry leader in AcroDance teacher training and certification. Learn more at AcrobaticArts.com.

Why acro?

Both sides of the body

Trains both sides of the body.

Safer on any stage

Safer transitions for performing on non-sprung stages.

Safe at every level

Developmentally appropriate safety at every level.

Built to last

Progressions that build alignment, strength, flexibility and control to protect dancers’ bodies into adulthood.

Cheer-ready

A strong foundation for cheerleading skills.

Artistry and strength

Develops both artistry and strength.

More exciting choreography

Adds excitement to lyrical, jazz and hip hop choreography.
