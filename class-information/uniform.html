Dance uniform and dress code

Every uniform item comes from the studio, and we size your dancer for you. No guesswork, no hunting online.

Why a uniform?

  • Convenience. We size your child and order the right fit every time.
  • Price. Dancewear is expensive. We chose items that balance cost and quality.
  • Team. When dancers dress like a team, they act like a team from day one.
  • Double duty. The purple leotard often becomes part of a recital costume, which keeps costume costs down.

For class, dancers need hair in a neat bun, a clean uniform and tights, and the right shoes for the day’s style. A nude leotard is required for every dancer except Dance With Me, for picture day and recitals.
