More than a recital

Every show tells a story, and every dancer has a place in it.

An original story

Almost every recital is a full story, usually original and written around our own dancers.

Room to lead

Dancers ready for a bigger challenge earn lead roles, building confidence and real leadership.

Brave counts

Our youngest dancers are supported on stage, whether they shine or shed a few tears. Every dancer receives an award for being brave enough to step out.

The road to recital

A 15-week semester, and every class counts.

  1. Weeks 1–4Technique

    Skills and confidence in each style.

  2. Week 5Choreography starts

    Each class begins learning its dance.

  3. Weeks 10–12Choreography set

    The full dance is in place.

  4. Final weeksClean & costumes

    Polish, costume try-ons and fit adjustments.

  5. Before the showPicture day

    Costume photos and final checks. Required.

  6. Show dayRecital

    Every class takes the stage.

Why attendance matters

Costume fittings

We try on costumes in class, make adjustments and make sure each one works for the dance.

Safety in the dance

Group lifts and formation changes depend on every dancer being there to learn their spot.

Room for sick days

We set choreography early to allow for illness, but a full class helps us the most.

Picture day is a must

We photograph every dancer in costume. Backstage helpers use these photos to make sure every dancer makes it on stage. If your dancer can’t attend, please let the office know right away.

Things to know

Costumes

Each class has its own dance and its own costume.

Stage makeup

Every dancer wears makeup. Stage lights wash out faces, and makeup helps the audience see expressions.

Backstage

For dancers’ safety, only staff and approved volunteers are allowed backstage and in the green room.

Tickets

Everyone watching needs a ticket for each show. Ticket sales keep the State Theatre running for our community.

Questions? We’re here to help.

Tell us about your dancer and we’ll reply with best-fit classes, usually within one business day.
