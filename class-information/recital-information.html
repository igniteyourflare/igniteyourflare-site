Classes

Recitals at the State Theatre

Two shows a year where every class performs its own dance in its own costume. It’s where all that practice shows.

Why we perform

Performing is a big part of the dance experience. It gives students a chance to show what they’ve worked on for four to five months, lets parents see how much their child has grown, and it’s a lot of fun. From Peewee up, dancers perform on their own without an older student or teacher helping on stage (exceptions always apply).

We expect dancers to practice at home. Dancers who only rehearse once a week in class struggle to remember the dance, and the goal is for them to know the steps well enough to really perform them.

Recitals usually follow a theme or storyline to make a cohesive show. There’s one recital fee per student no matter how many dances they’re in, and it’s included in all-inclusive tuition. Recital participation is required for semester classes.

Things to know

Picture day

A required event before each recital to check costume fit and stage makeup, and to take the photos used in the program.

Costumes

Each class has its own dance and its own costume.

Stage makeup

Every dancer wears makeup. Stage lights wash out faces, and makeup helps the audience see expressions.

Backstage

For dancers’ safety, only staff and approved volunteers are allowed backstage and in the green room.

Tickets

Everyone watching needs a ticket for each show. Ticket sales keep the State Theatre running for our community.

Phones away

Every show is professionally recorded, and the video download is included in tuition, so you can sit back and watch.
