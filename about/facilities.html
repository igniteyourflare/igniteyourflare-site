About

Our studios in downtown Red Bluff

Two dance rooms, both with sprung floors to protect dancers through their training, and an office on the corner of Walnut and Jefferson.

The office and store

Our main entrance is on Jefferson St.: look for the sunset ombre door. This is where you get fitted for uniforms and can talk to staff about any questions.

Studio A

On the corner of Walnut and Jefferson, set up for acro, camps and special events, with a sprung floor, individual and large tumbling mats, a tumble track and more.

Studio B

Right next door, with mirrors, wall-mounted and portable barres and a sprung floor. Ideal for ballet, and home to our dance tech yoga balls.

The studio has multiple cameras to encourage dancers’ independence while giving parents peace of mind. There’s no waiting room, and parents can watch class live online through Band.

Take a look inside

Sprung floors in both studios

Floors that give a little with every jump and landing, to protect dancers through their training.
