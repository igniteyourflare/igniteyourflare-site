Introduction

Ignite Your Flare: Dance & Acrobatics (“Ignite Your Flare,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you visit our website, register for classes or events, contact us, participate in our programs, or otherwise interact with our services.

By accessing or using our website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Contact us through our website, text, phone or email
  • Complete an inquiry, registration or enrollment form
  • Enroll in classes, camps, workshops, teams, performances or events
  • Subscribe to newsletters or marketing communications
  • Complete waivers, contracts, surveys or questionnaires
  • Submit payments or purchase gift cards, merchandise or uniform items

This information may include:

  • Parent or guardian name
  • Student name, birthday, gender and dance experience
  • Email address, phone number and mailing address
  • Emergency contact information
  • Enrollment, attendance and class placement information
  • Signed agreements and waivers
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communication preferences and opt-in records, including timestamps for SMS and email consent
  • Any additional information you choose to provide

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type, device information and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring websites and traffic sources
  • General geographic location (city or region)
  • Website interaction and engagement data, such as clicks on phone, text and email links

This information is collected through cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies, described below.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide classes, camps, performances and other services
  • Respond to inquiries and recommend classes
  • Process registrations, enrollments, payments and billing
  • Communicate with students and families by email, text, phone and the BAND app
  • Manage studio operations, records and safety
  • Improve our website and services
  • Analyze website performance and engagement
  • Measure advertising effectiveness and deliver relevant advertising, including remarketing
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud
  • Keep records of your communication preferences and consent
  • Comply with legal obligations

Cookies, Pixels and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, pixels, tags, embedded content and similar technologies from providers including Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, YouTube and Cloudflare. These technologies may be used to:

  • Keep the website secure and working properly
  • Analyze website traffic and understand how visitors use our website
  • Measure advertising performance and website conversions
  • Deliver relevant advertisements and show ads to previous website visitors (remarketing)
  • Support embedded video and other content

Your cookie choices

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner lets you Accept or Decline analytics and advertising cookies. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of every page. If you decline, we turn off Google Analytics and advertising cookies through Google Consent Mode; essential features of the site continue to work.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage advertising preferences through the platforms listed below. Some features, forms, videos or advertising functions may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

Global Privacy Control

Our website honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. If your browser sends a GPC signal, we automatically treat it as a request to opt out of advertising cookies and the sharing of your information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Google tag gateway

Some Google measurement tags are delivered through our own website domain using Cloudflare’s Google tag gateway. This changes where the tag loads from, not what it collects, and your cookie choices still apply.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors interact with our website, including pages visited, time on pages, device and browser type, general region and traffic sources. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to provide these services.

Learn how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Advertising and Remarketing

We use, or may use, advertising and measurement services from Google (Google Ads), Meta (Facebook and Instagram, including the Meta Pixel) and TikTok (including the TikTok Pixel). These services may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to measure ad effectiveness, track website conversions, build advertising audiences, deliver personalized ads and show ads to previous visitors across other websites, apps and platforms.

You can opt out at any time with our Cookie Settings link or a GPC signal, and manage your preferences directly with each platform:

  • Google Ads Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Meta Ad Preferences: facebook.com/adpreferences
  • TikTok ad settings: tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Industry opt-out tools: optout.aboutads.info

Service Providers We Use

We use trusted service providers to operate our website, registration, payments, communications, advertising, analytics and studio operations. Depending on the service, these may include:

Website hosting, security and analytics

  • Cloudflare (website hosting, security, performance and Google tag gateway)
  • GitHub (website code storage)
  • Google Analytics 4
  • Google Ads
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram)
  • TikTok Pixel

Forms, registration and records

  • Airtable (inquiry forms and studio records)
  • Tally (online forms)
  • JotForm (online forms)
  • DocHub (documents and e-signatures)
  • HighLevel (customer relationship management, inquiry follow-up, email and text messaging)

Payments and billing

  • Helcim (invoices, card on file, online store and gift cards)
  • Square
  • Stripe

Communication and operations

  • BAND (family and class communication)
  • Sked Social (social media scheduling)
  • Google Workspace and Google Drive (email and file storage)

Video and embedded content

  • YouTube and Google Maps embeds

These providers may collect, process, store or secure personal information on our behalf as needed to provide their services, such as contact details, registration and payment information, enrollment records, signed documents, communication records and website activity data. Each provider has its own privacy practices, and we encourage you to review them. Payment card numbers are handled by our payment processors and are not stored on our website.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We share personal information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide requested services, process payments and manage enrollments
  • Communicate with families
  • Operate our business through the service providers listed above
  • Measure and deliver advertising, subject to your cookie choices
  • Comply with legal obligations, legal process or valid law enforcement requests
  • Protect the rights, safety and security of our students, families, staff and studio
  • Complete a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of assets, in which case your information remains protected under this policy

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages (for example, by checking the SMS consent box on our inquiry form), you agree to receive texts related to your inquiry and our services, such as class information, appointment and enrollment reminders, customer support and important studio updates. Promotional texts are sent only if you separately opt in.

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You’ll receive one confirmation and no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us at office@igniteyourflare.com or (530) 255-4180.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as customer service or the platforms that deliver our messages, is permitted. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Student Photos and Media

Photos, videos, audio recordings and other media may be created during classes, camps, performances, competitions, workshops and studio events. As described in our Policies & Fees Handbook and enrollment agreement, Ignite Your Flare may use this media for educational, promotional, marketing, archival and social media purposes, including on our website, social media, advertising, event programs and newsletters.

When we feature students online, such as on our Team Dancers page, we use first names and last initials only and avoid publishing sensitive personal information alongside student media. Parents or guardians may contact us at any time with questions about media use or to update previously provided permissions.

Children’s Privacy

Ignite Your Flare primarily serves children and families. Information about students is provided by a parent or legal guardian. Our website is intended for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information to us without a parent’s involvement, contact us and we will delete it.

Parents or guardians may contact us to review the information associated with their child, request corrections, or request deletion, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) on our website and access controls on our systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, fulfill contracts, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymize it.

California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and share
  • Access a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information, where applicable
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

We do not sell personal information for money. Our use of advertising cookies and pixels for remarketing may be considered “sharing” under California law. You can opt out at any time with our Cookie Settings link or a Global Privacy Control signal.

To make a request, email office@igniteyourflare.com or call (530) 255-4180. We may need to verify your identity before responding. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. Under California’s “Shine the Light” law, you may also ask whether we share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing; we do not.

Your Choices

  • Update or correct your information by contacting us
  • Opt out of marketing emails with the “unsubscribe” link in any email
  • Opt out of texts by replying STOP
  • Change your cookie choices with the Cookie Settings link
  • Withdraw consent for future communications at any time

Do Not Track

There is no common industry standard for browser “Do Not Track” signals, so our website does not respond to them. We do honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as described above.

Third-Party Websites

Our website links to third-party websites and services, such as our payment portal, online store, BAND and partner organizations. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, content or security, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. For significant changes, we may also notify you by email or with a notice on our website. Continued use of our website after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
